# Chennai Public Transport Data Attribution

## Data Source
The static public transport data used in Tour Companion (Semmozhi) is derived from the community-maintained **ChennaiGTFS** project:
- Repository: [ungalsoththu/ChennaiGTFS](https://github.com/ungalsoththu/ChennaiGTFS)
- Dataset: `chennai-unified-gtfs.zip`

## Underlying Data Rights & Disclaimer
- **Metropolitan Transport Corporation (MTC) Chennai:** Bus routes, schedules, and stop names remain under the purview and rights of MTC Chennai.
- **Chennai Metro Rail Limited (CMRL):** Metro lines, stations, and transfer schedules remain under the purview and rights of CMRL.
- **Southern Railway (SR / Indian Railways):** Chennai suburban rail and MRTS routes, stations, and alignments remain under the purview and rights of Southern Railway.

## Important Project Notices
- This static data is a community-compiled compilation for offline and general traveler transit navigation within Chennai.
- This application does not claim official endorsement, sponsorship, or partnership with MTC, CMRL, Southern Railway, or the Government of Tamil Nadu.
- Schedules, routes, and operational stops are subject to operational changes by the respective public transit authorities.
